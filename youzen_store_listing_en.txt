youZen - Your YouTube, But Softer

youZen is a free Chrome extension that quiets YouTube. Hide Shorts, silence
comments, and switch off the endless "up next" pull — so you watch what you
came for and nothing else fights for your attention.

One side panel. A small pile of switches. Flip what you want quiet.

🎯 WHAT IT DOES:

  🙈 Hide Shorts Everywhere
     Remove Shorts from the homepage, sidebar, search, channel pages, and
     your Subscriptions feed. One master switch, or pick surface by surface.

  🧹 Declutter the Homepage
     Replace the endless algorithmic feed with a tidy quick-links card,
     send yourself straight to Subscriptions, or leave it calmly empty.

  📺 Calm the Watch Page
     Hide comments, the related-videos sidebar, end-screen cards, autoplay,
     merch shelves, and like / dislike counts — each its own simple switch.

  ↩️ Skip the Shorts Player
     Open a Shorts link and land on the normal video page instead, with
     full controls and no vertical-scroll trap.

  🚫 Block Keywords & Channels
     Two simple block lists hide videos by keyword or by channel. Just
     right-click any text on YouTube to block it instantly.

  ⏸️ Pause Anytime
     Switch youZen off for one tab or everywhere at once. Your settings
     stay exactly as you left them.

  🌙 Light & Dark Theme
     Follows your system by default, and remembers your choice.

  🔒 Truly Private
     No tracking, no accounts, no sign-up. Nothing ever leaves your browser.
     Free and open source — every line is public.

✅ SIMPLE TO USE:
  1. Install youZen and open the side panel.
  2. Flip the switches for what you want gone.
  3. Enjoy a quieter YouTube.

No account. No setup. No cost.
